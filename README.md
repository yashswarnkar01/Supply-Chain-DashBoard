# Supply-Chain-Dasgboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing supply chain performance, inventory, transportation modes, supplier efficiency, costs, product quality, and operational trends to support data-driven decisions.

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. It focuses on analyzing supply chain costs, product performance, transportation modes, supplier efficiency, defect rates, and order quantities.

The dashboard helps users understand operational performance and identify areas for improvement in supply chain management.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze supply chain costs across different locations.
- Monitor product quality and defect rates.
- Evaluate transportation mode performance.
- Analyze order quantities and product demand.
- Compare suppliers and supply chain operations.
- Support data-driven operational decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard focuses on the following metrics:
- Total SKUs
- Total Cost
- Average Defect Rate
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Revenue

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Average Defect Rate by Product
A stacked column chart used to compare defect rates across products and identify potential quality issues.

### 2. Total Quantity Sold by Transportation Mode
A stacked bar chart showing order quantities across transportation modes to compare their contribution to product movement.

### 3. Total Cost by Location
A chart comparing supply chain costs across locations to identify areas with higher operating expenses.

### 4. Total SKUs
A funnel chart displaying SKU-related information to provide an overview of the products covered in the analysis.

### 5. Total Cost and Quantity by Location
A scatter chart comparing costs and quantities across locations to explore relationships between spending and order volume.

### 6. Location and Route Analysis
A matrix displaying locations and different routes to support comparisons of supply chain operations.

## Interactive Features
- Slicers for routes, locations, suppliers, and product types.
- Interactive charts for exploring supply chain metrics.
- Location-wise and transportation-wise comparisons.
- Multiple visuals for operational performance analysis.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard creation and visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and measures.
- **Data Modeling:** Organizing data for analysis.

## Business Value
This dashboard helps businesses monitor costs, understand transportation patterns, evaluate product quality, and analyze order quantities. These insights can support better inventory planning, cost control, and supply chain decision-making.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform supply chain data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, and comparative analysis. It supports a better understanding of supply chain costs, quality, transportation, and operational performance.
